Cookies & local storage

A small, practical footprint.

Public website

The public pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Functional session storage keeps your selected equipment and business need across pages in the current browser tab. Contact details and free-text answers are not saved. You can clear the list yourself; closing the tab clears the saved session. There is no customer or administrator login.

Form submissions

Sending a form takes you to FormSubmit for processing and spam prevention. Its service and Google reCAPTCHA may use cookies or similar technology. Read FormSubmit’s privacy policy for details.

Other providers

GitHub Pages serves the website. Fonts are served directly with the website. Opening WhatsApp moves the conversation to that service. These providers process technical requests under their own policies.

Your controls

Browser settings let you clear or block cookies. Blocking spam-prevention functionality may prevent form submission; you can instead email chimaster010@gmail.com directly. Last updated: 16 October 2026.
