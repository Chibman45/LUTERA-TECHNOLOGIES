# Current brand and motion

The public rebrand adopts the supplied Lab rows identity. The exact horizontal master SVG is used on light and dark grounds, with the supplied favicon and regenerated share artwork. Typography and colours come from the design-system archive: self-hosted Sora, occasional Playfair italic, Paper, Navy and blue, with signal cyan reserved for dark surfaces.

The owner approved replacing the former silver L artwork and 3D equipment hero. The Three.js renderer, library, hotspots, old illustrations and associated eligibility tests have been retired. Equipment discovery now uses semantic tabs and relevant local photographs. Short state transitions support hover, focus and selection; reduced-motion preferences disable them. Navigation and service sections keep their content visible and use short state transitions. The photographic hero’s optional motion has separate controls and reduced-motion support.

All services receive equal card widths and shared styling. Labs are included under installation and technical support. Navigation, forms and floating contact disclosure retain no-JavaScript access. See the browser tests and rebrand review for verification.

## Photographic hero follow-up

The owner requested a transitioning photographic background, with the hero text remaining visible over the photographs. This adds a scoped image crossfade and slow pan, plus working previous/next and pause/resume controls. A constant Navy overlay maintains text contrast through every blend. The supplied logo, service prominence and remaining page layouts retain the approved rebrand direction.

Open Design was explicitly requested, but its local runtime/MCP connection was unavailable in this workspace. The existing site was edited directly; this update is not represented as an Open Design-generated artifact.

Reduced-motion, data-saving and no-JavaScript visits use a still initial image; manual previous/next remains available when JavaScript is enabled. Automatic playback and panning stop offscreen or while the tab is hidden. Missing secondary images leave the current image visible. The native enquiry flow was also checked: transferred equipment selections are consumed after successful session storage so removed items do not return on reload. Storage-blocked visits retain the URL fallback.
