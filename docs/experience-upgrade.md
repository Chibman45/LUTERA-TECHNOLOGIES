# Public enquiry experience

The rebrand preserves the useful enquiry features while applying the supplied visual system:

- An equipment explorer with keyboard-operable tabs, category-specific photographs and quote actions.
- Four starting-point business needs and a clickable project-process timeline.
- An equipment enquiry list with quantities, removal, copying and cross-page session persistence.
- A comparison table with selectable columns and contained horizontal scrolling.
- A three-step quote form with validation, future-date checks, review and a structured email summary.
- Service-specific quote links for all eight services; digital work does not require equipment quantities.
- A native contact form and a bottom-right disclosure for quote, enquiry list and WhatsApp.
- Fresh-visit cookie choices with footer settings, storage-blocked fallbacks and no optional tracking.

Both forms submit over HTTPS to FormSubmit for chimaster010@gmail.com. Browser tests intercept requests to verify the payload and redirect without sending real enquiries. With JavaScript disabled, all quote stages remain visible and native submission works. No contact/free-text data is stored in the browser enquiry list.

The former 3D hotspots, orbit graphics and equipment popups have been removed with the owner-approved design change. The semantic equipment tabs provide the discovery interaction on every device.
