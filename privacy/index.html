Privacy notice

How we handle your enquiry.

Who to contact

LUTERA TECHNOLOGIES is the working brand behind this website; incorporation is pending. For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact chimaster010@gmail.com.

Information you provide

Quote enquiries request your organization, contact name, email, category and requirements. Phone, budget, location, timing and notes are optional, except quantities for equipment enquiries. The contact form asks for your name, email and message, with organization optional. Do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive documents.

Purpose and basis

We use the information you send to clarify requirements, prepare quotations and respond to your business enquiry. Processing supports steps you request before a contract and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, subject to applicable law. Acknowledging this notice is separate from marketing consent. This website does not collect marketing subscriptions.

Email processing and service providers

Both forms send the submitted fields to FormSubmit, which processes them and routes them to chimaster010@gmail.com. FormSubmit is a third-party form service, not a database operated by LUTERA. It may retain submissions or technical logs under its own policy. Review FormSubmit’s privacy policy. Its spam-prevention checks may use Google reCAPTCHA. Email is processed by Google’s Gmail service. GitHub Pages serves this website and may process request information. Fonts are hosted with the website, without requests to Google Fonts. Clicking WhatsApp opens a separate third-party service.

Access, retention and international processing

Enquiries are reviewed through the business email inbox. Information is retained only as needed for the enquiry, an agreed transaction and relevant legal obligations. You can request deletion using the contact email; third-party providers may retain logs or backups according to their policies. Providers may process information outside Nigeria. No private customer account or RFQ database is operated by this website.

Your rights

Contact the email above to request access, correction, deletion or information about processing. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. This notice describes the workflow and does not claim certification or automatic legal compliance.

Website storage and updates

The website uses no advertising analytics or administrator login. The equipment enquiry list and selected business need are kept in session storage for the current browser tab. Contact details and free-text form answers are not saved in browser storage. You can clear the equipment list at any time; closing the tab clears the saved session. Spam-prevention services may use their own cookies on the submission page. Last updated: 16 October 2026.
