About LUTERA

Technology with a business purpose.

Based in Abuja, LUTERA TECHNOLOGIES brings together IT and AI solutions, software development, hardware supply and practical technology consulting around your business needs.

Our story

Moving organizations forward, one decision at a time.

From improving a business process to connecting systems or equipping a workplace, LUTERA brings technology conversations together. We help organizations define their requirements and consider suitable options.

Our work spans AI solutions and agents, business process automation, software design and implementation, data analytics, systems integration and cloud-based solutions. We also provide technology consultancy, hardware procurement and supply, installation, maintenance and technical support. Project scope, equipment availability and delivery arrangements are confirmed for each enquiry.
